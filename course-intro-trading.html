About This Course

This foundational course is designed for absolute beginners eager to understand the basics of trading. We'll demystify financial markets, explain key terminology, and introduce you to the core concepts of buying and selling assets. You'll gain a solid understanding of market participants, order types, and how to interpret basic market data. This course is essential for anyone looking to build a strong trading foundation.

We cover everything from stocks and bonds to commodities and forex, providing a holistic view of the trading landscape. You'll learn about the different types of exchanges, how prices are formed, and the basic tools used by traders to analyze market conditions. This course sets the stage for more advanced learning.

What You'll Learn

  • Understand financial market structure
  • Identify different types of trading assets
  • Learn essential trading terminology
  • Grasp the concept of supply and demand
  • Execute basic buy and sell orders
  • Introduction to market analysis

Course Modules

Module 1: What are Financial Markets?

Covers the definition, purpose, and types of financial markets.

Module 2: Types of Assets & Instruments

Covers stocks, bonds, commodities, forex, and cryptocurrencies.

Module 3: Key Trading Terminology

Covers bids, asks, spreads, lots, leverage, and margin.

Module 4: Market Participants & Their Roles

Covers retail traders, institutional investors, market makers, and brokers.

Module 5: Understanding Order Types

Covers market orders, limit orders, stop orders, and their strategic use.

Module 6: Introduction to Market Data

Covers reading price charts, basic indicators, and economic calendars.
